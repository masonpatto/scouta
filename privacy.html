Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Scouta is a personal, independently-run project. This page explains what information we collect when you use it, why, and what rights you have over it. If anything here is unclear, email us — the address is at the bottom.

Who we are

Scouta is operated by an individual developer, not a company. "We" and "us" below refers to that operator. Scouta is not affiliated with any football club, league, players' association, or the data providers it uses — see the Terms of Use for more on that.

What we collect

When you create an account:

  • Your email address
  • A password — this is hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store your actual password

As you use the app:

  • Your scout profile: casual/professional mode, your scouting archetype quiz answers
  • Your in-app activity: players you buy, sell, or watchlist, your Scout Coin balance and transaction history
  • Content you write yourself: Scout Journal entries you create about players

Automatically, to keep the app working:

  • Basic usage events (e.g. which features get used) to help us understand what's working and what isn't
  • If something breaks: a crash report including device/browser type, the error itself, and what you were doing when it happened

We do not collect payment details, government ID, biometric data, or precise location. There is no real money anywhere in Scouta — see the Terms of Use.

Why we collect it

  • To create and run your account
  • To make the app work the way it's supposed to (your portfolio, leaderboard position, journal, etc. all depend on this data existing)
  • To find and fix bugs
  • To understand which parts of the app are actually used, so we know what to improve

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data, to anyone, ever. We do use a small number of infrastructure providers to run the app, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication, and all of your account data
  • Sentry — receives crash/error reports only, so we can find and fix bugs; this does not include your trading activity or journal content unless that specific data happened to be involved in the error

Player names, clubs, and ratings shown in the app come from a football-data provider (Sportmonks) and EA Sports FC — that's public information about real footballers, not about you, and no data about you is ever sent to them.

Data may be processed on servers outside your own country, depending on where these providers run their infrastructure.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your profile, holdings, transaction history, watchlist, and journal entries are permanently deleted — this happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Your rights

  • Delete your account — do this yourself anytime from Profile → Delete my account, or email us and we'll do it for you
  • Access or export your data — email us and we'll send you a copy
  • Correct inaccurate data — most of your own data (profile, journal) is already editable in the app; email us for anything that isn't
  • Object to how your data is used — email us

Children

Scouta is not intended for anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Continuing to use Scouta after a change means you accept the updated policy.
