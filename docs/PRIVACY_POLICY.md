## Who this policy covers

Scouta ("the App") is developed and operated by [Your Name / Company
Name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect when you
use Scouta, why, and what control you have over it.

## What Scouta is

Scouta is a football scouting and talent-discovery game. You build a
virtual portfolio of footballers using SC, a fictional in-game currency
with no real-world monetary value. Scouta is not a financial, investment,
or gambling product — see [docs/PRODUCT.md] for the full product
positioning.

## Information we collect

**Account information you provide:**
- Email address (used for login and account recovery)
- Password (never stored in plain text — handled by our authentication
  provider, Supabase Auth, using industry-standard hashing)
- A scout name you choose during onboarding (a display name, not
  required to be your real name)

**Gameplay data generated by using the app:**
- Your SC balance, holdings, buy/sell transaction history, watchlist,
  and milestone/achievement progress
- Your Casual/Professional mode selection

**What we do NOT collect:**
- We do not collect your real name, address, or phone number
- We do not access your device's location, camera, microphone, or
  contacts
- We do not currently use any third-party advertising or analytics
  SDKs
- Your onboarding quiz answers are used on your device to calculate
  your Scout archetype and never sent to or stored on our servers —
  only the resulting scout name and mode selection are saved

## How we use your information

- To create and secure your account
- To operate the core gameplay loop (track your SC balance, holdings,
  and transaction history)
- To show leaderboards (only your chosen scout name and your ROI% are
  shown to other users — your email and real balance are never shown to
  anyone else)
- To send you in-app notifications about players you hold or watch
  (price moves, injury news) and your own milestones

## Who we share data with

- **Supabase**, our backend/database provider, which stores your
  account and gameplay data on our behalf as a data processor.
- We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
- Player statistics used to price players in the game come from public
  football data APIs (currently the Fantasy Premier League API); no
  personal data about you is sent to these services.

## Your controls

- You can log out at any time.
- You can request a password reset from the login screen.
- **You can permanently delete your account and all associated data**
  from the Profile screen ("Delete my account"). This immediately and
  irreversibly removes your profile, holdings, transaction history,
  watchlist, and notifications.

## Children's privacy

Scouta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a
child has created an account, contact us at [support email] and we will
delete it.
